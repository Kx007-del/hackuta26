FaZe Clan: A Timeline, 2010–2026
Source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HI-h6v38zGZaYzV_yc1G7u0NIec8pBerYdqO3z6mjQ4

FaZe Clan: A Timeline, 2010–2026

01  2010-11 — FaZe Sniping: three kids and an Intervention

2010

May 2010 · Founded as FaZe Sniping

Eric “ClipZ” Rivera, Jeff “House Cat” Emann and Ben “Resistance” Christensen start a Call of Duty trickshotting crew on YouTube. [Wikipedia]

May 31, 2010 · The first upload — “FaZe Sniping Intro”

475,099 lifetime views. The channel goes live the day after the crew forms: three players, one Intervention, and no plan beyond the next clip. A “FaZe Clan Minitage” and ILLCAMS Episode 1 both land within seventy-two hours. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Jun 2, 2010 · ILLCAMS Episode 1

1.40M lifetime views, uploaded three days after the channel opened — not months later, as most retrospectives have it. The killcam-compilation format becomes the channel's subscriber engine for four straight years and runs to Episode 49 before going dark. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Aug 3, 2010 · Introducing FaZe Temperrr

1.62M views on the intro video. Thomas “Temperrr” Oliveira joins at sixteen, brings the graphic-design skills that produce the logo — his concept, drawn up by FaZe designer Joey “Ferox” Ricciuto, a reversed F overlapping a C — invents the shot that carries his name, and ends up the org's president. Earlier versions of this page dated his arrival to July on a year-level source; the channel's own introduction video puts it here. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Sept 2010 · Temperrr invents his signature no-scope shot

Thomas “Temperrr” Oliveira originates a jump-spin, fire-without-scoping sniper trick that the community starts calling the “Temperrr Shot” — one of the earliest tricks to carry a FaZe member's own name. [Tubefilter]

Nov 6, 2010 · OpTic's H3CZ turns up on the FaZe channel

“OpTic H3CZ Meets FaZe!”, 285K views. Two and a half years before the two orgs first meet in a bracket, Call of Duty's defining rivalry starts life as a crossover video — which is roughly how H3CZ has always described it. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Dec 16, 2010 · “Our War” — the first teamtage

576,571 views, a Black Ops Search and Destroy team montage teased since Nov 18. The teamtage — the whole roster's clips cut to one track and dropped like a record — begins here. Roughly fifty follow over the next fifteen years. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Dec 22, 2010 · House Cat becomes FaZe Timid

“Re-Introducing FaZe Timid.” Co-founder Jeff Emann retires the name the org was founded under. Of the three founders, he is the one whose alias most fans never learn. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2011

Jan 22, 2011 · Introducing FaZe Fakie

2.46M views on the intro alone. Ted “Fakie” becomes the channel's most prolific personality — “Fakie has Game!!” runs 38 episodes — and the on-camera voice FaZe puts forward whenever it has to answer for itself. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Jan 31, 2011 · “How to Temperrr Shot” does 5.75M views

A tutorial, not a montage, and the most-watched video of FaZe's first two years. The move gets a name, the name gets a how-to, and a generation of Xbox 360 players learns it from the person it is named after. “How to Top Spin” with Fakie follows on Feb 6 (1.52M). [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Feb 28, 2011 · FaZe Top 5 Trickshots, Episode 1

2.15M views, hosted by Temperrr. A weekly leaderboard for the community's best clips — FaZe positioning itself as the scene's judge rather than just another crew in it. The series runs, on and off, for years. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Mar 6, 2011 · FaZe Apex joins on his fourteenth birthday

Yousef “Apex” Abdelfattah joins, by his own account, the day he turned 14; the channel's introduction video follows on Mar 19 and does 1.60M views. He becomes COO, lands the first Black Ops 2 trickshot killcam, and leaves in the Christmas 2025 exodus — a little under fifteen years later. [FaZe Apex on X]

Mar 27, 2011 · The community montage franchise begins

“The Community TrickShot Montage Trailer,” 273K views on the trailer alone. FaZe starts cutting montages from clips sent in by people who are not in FaZe — the single smartest thing the org did in its first two years, and the reason the scene grew up around it rather than beside it. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Apr 3, 2011 · “FaZe Does Not Set Up Clips”

802K views. Accused of staging trickshots in rigged private lobbies, the crew answers with a Fakie commentary defending the clips as legitimate. The first time FaZe has to defend its own credibility on camera — and the template for every response video that follows. [FaZe Clan YouTube — upload no longer public]

Apr 10, 2011 · 100,000 subscribers

Marked with a video titled “FaZe thanks you for 100k!!!!!!!!!!!” — eleven exclamation marks, eleven months after the first upload. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

May 31, 2011 · “Historic: A Community Montage,” on the first anniversary

302K views, released a year to the day after the first upload. ILLFEEDS Episode 1 (452K) lands the same day — a second franchise spun out of the ILLCAMS format. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Jun 21, 2011 · Introducing FaZe Spratt

James “Spratt” joins and becomes the defining sniper of the Modern Warfare 3 era — fourteen prestige montages, the EXAMPLE series, and the first MW3 sniper montage on the channel. Twelve years later FaZe asks him back to lead a revived sniping division; the layoffs kill it before it launches. Watch the intro video. [GGRecon]

Jul 11, 2011 · “Sniping With Style” Episode 1

836K views. Another format built to showcase the roster collectively rather than one member at a time. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Jul 29, 2011 · PARALLEL — Fakie and Temperrr's dualtage

3.30M views, edited by MinK and trailed since May 14 (the trailer alone did 1.22M). The dualtage as an event: two members, one montage, months of build-up. Nothing on the channel outside the Temperrr Shot tutorial touches it until the 1M teamtage. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Aug 13, 2011 · Introducing FaZe Heist

205,871 views. Heist runs the “No Witnesses” series and two of the era's better-remembered montages — STASIS in November 2011 and DEVIATE, edited by MinK, in October 2012. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Aug 19, 2011 · 300,000 subscribers

588,635 views on the thank-you video — more than five times the subscriber count it was celebrating. Four months from 100K to 300K. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Sept 14, 2011 · “Legendary: A Community Montage”

228,959 views, edited by FaZe Agony — the third entry in the community-montage line after March's trailer and May's “Historic,” and the one longtime fans still name first. Its own description places it in a sequence with an earlier entry, “Belief,” which does not survive on the main channel. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Oct 20, 2011 · Introducing FaZe Ramos

377,544 views. Ramos becomes one of the most imitated trickshotters of the era — enough that a shot ends up carrying his name, the way Temperrr's did. His “Go On Ramos!” series runs sixteen episodes across four Call of Duty titles. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Oct 26, 2011 · “Clash of the Clan” — 400,000 subscribers

586,812 views. An intra-roster tournament built as a subscriber-milestone event; it comes back for the two-year anniversary in 2012 and as North America vs Europe in 2013. FaZe competing against itself, on camera, years before it competed against anyone else. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Oct 27, 2011 · Hoodie Allen guests on “Fakie has Game”

Episode 35, 442K views. The first rapper crossover on the channel — and the same artist FaZe brings back three years later to headline ILLCAMS 49. Music-adjacent celebrity was in the formula from 2011, not from the Sports Illustrated era. [FaZe Clan YouTube — upload no longer public]

Nov 10, 2011 · Modern Warfare 3 Teamtage #1

1.60M views, three days after the game launched. Seven numbered MW3 teamtages follow through July 2012 — the most sustained numbered teamtage run the org ever managed, and evidence that the “no numbered series” claim only holds for the milestone tages, not the game-specific ones. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Nov 13, 2011 · Spratt's first MW3 sniper montage

1.05M views, six days into the new game's life cycle. Being first mattered more than being best: FaZe's whole growth pattern in this era is claiming a new title's sniping meta before anyone else has finished installing it. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

02  2012-13 — A million subscribers, and a first run at the pros

2012

Jan 7, 2012 · ILLCAMS 36, and 500,000 subscribers

1.79M views, with the milestone folded into the episode title rather than given its own video. Nineteen months from launch to half a million. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Feb 7, 2012 · Introducing FaZe Twistt

392,834 views. Worth separating from a much later name: this is FaZe Twistt the trickshotter, no relation to Russel “Twistzz” Van Dulken, the Counter-Strike rifler FaZe signs in 2021. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Feb 21, 2012 · The first MW3 crane shot killcam

971,201 views, hit by FaZe Force. The “first on a new map or mechanic” video becomes a reliable million-view format all by itself — the channel ran the same play for Sanctuary, Uplink, Dig and the Ghosts launch. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Feb 25, 2012 · ILLCAMS Episode 38 — the real series peak, at 5.44M

Edited by MinK, 5,441,508 lifetime views, and the highest-viewed ILLCAMS ever made by a wide margin. An earlier version of this page gave that title to Episode 49; the catalogue does not support it. Episodes 39 (3.31M), 37 (2.89M), 40 (2.89M) and 43 (2.44M) all out-perform 49's 2.33M as well. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Mar 8, 2012 · Introducing FaZe Pamaj

850,248 views. Austin Pamajewon joins and becomes, on the numbers, the single biggest draw of the trickshot era — his Black Ops 2 and MW2/MW3 montages are the two most-watched member videos the channel ever published. An earlier version of this page carried a “correction” claiming Pamaj was never on the FaZe roster and belonged entirely to OpTic. That was wrong in both directions, and it is retracted here: he starts at FaZe. Watch the intro video. [Dexerto]

Mar 18, 2012 · Pamaj: Iridescent 3

1.65M views, ten days after his introduction video. The fastest any member has gone from joining to carrying the channel. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Apr 28, 2012 · WaRTeK: World War III

1.15M views. Anil “WaRTeK” Brancaleoni is already the biggest French name on the roster seventeen months before FaZe builds an entire European division around him. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

May 2, 2012 · Introducing FaZe zejiM

361,507 views. zejiM's mid-air tagshot is still passed around as one of the defining clips of the Black Ops and MW2 sniping era — the kind of shot that gets re-uploaded by nostalgia accounts a decade later without anyone needing to caption it. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

May 30, 2012 · Clash of the Clan 2 — two years of FaZe

720,820 views, dropped on the anniversary. The org is two years old, has more than half a million subscribers, and has still never entered a professional tournament. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2012 · G FUEL partnership begins

One of the org's longest-running and most fan-associated brand deals, still active over a decade later. [Wikipedia]

Aug 18, 2012 · Spratt: EXAMPLE 3

847,157 views. The EXAMPLE line is the closest thing the sniping side had to a prestige franchise under one member's name; EXAMPLE 4 follows in August 2013 at 1.53M. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2012 · First turn toward competitive esports

The crew starts recruiting competitive Call of Duty players alongside its content side, aiming at the Call of Duty Championship. [Tubefilter]

Sept 3, 2012 · Pamaj: THE CATALYST

5,363,906 views — at the time the biggest single-member video FaZe had ever released, and still third on the all-time list behind only the 1M teamtage and Pamaj's own Black Ops 2 montage two months later. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Sept 28, 2012 · CONFLICTION — Ramos and zejiM's dualtage

1,722,910 views, edited by Meek and trailed since Aug 24. The biggest dualtage since PARALLEL fourteen months earlier, and the clearest example of the format at its peak: two of the roster's most technical snipers, one release date, a month of build. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Oct 8, 2012 · The 1 Million Subscribers Teamtage — 12.1M views, and the biggest video FaZe ever made

Edited by MinK. At 12,134,253 lifetime views it is more than double the next-biggest teamtage and the most-watched upload in the channel's history, in any era, by any member. An earlier version of this page called the 2013 2M Subscribers Teamtage (5.47M) the biggest ever. It is second. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Oct 12, 2012 · Introducing FaZe Rain

490,526 views. Nordan “Rain” Shat becomes one of the channel's most-watched personalities and, by 2014, its biggest solo montage draw. Note the date: the org's own introduction video puts him at October 2012, not 2011, which is what several later retrospectives report. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Nov 2012 · Black Ops 2 sets off a second trickshotting boom

The new Call of Duty entry and its Ballista rifle trigger a fresh wave of viral clips, and FaZe takes the whole launch window: Pamaj's first BO2 sniper montage (8.0M), Apex's first BO2 trickshot killcam (1.7M) and BO2 Teamtage #1 (2.0M) all land inside 48 hours. An earlier version of this page used this entry to “correct” a claim that Pamaj was a FaZe name, asserting his fame ran entirely through OpTic. That was itself the error, and it is withdrawn — Pamaj was on FaZe from March 2012, and this is his window. Watch: Pamaj’s BO2 montage, Apex’s killcam, BO2 Teamtage 1. [Dexerto]

Nov 13, 2012 · Apex lands the first Black Ops 2 trickshot killcam

1.71M views. The same day as Pamaj's montage: FaZe took the first-on-a-new-game slot twice over, with the org's future COO getting one of them. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Nov 13, 2012 · Pamaj's first Black Ops 2 sniper montage — 8.0M views

8,005,940 lifetime views, published the day after the game's launch window opened. The second-most-watched video in FaZe history, behind only the 1M teamtage — and the single clearest illustration of how much of the trickshot era's reach ran through one person. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Nov 14, 2012 · Black Ops 2 Teamtage #1

2.00M views, forty-eight hours after the game arrived. Four numbered BO2 teamtages follow in six weeks. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Dec 16, 2012 · “Our War 2”

1.09M views, edited by Meek, two years to the day after the original. The only teamtage FaZe ever made a direct sequel to. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Dec 26, 2012 · The Christmas teamtage tradition actually starts here

“Black Ops 2 Teamtage #3 (Holiday Special!),” edited by MinK, 1,402,497 views — a year earlier than this page previously said, and comfortably the biggest of the five holiday drops. The run then goes 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016 before lapsing. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2013

Jan 2013 · FaZe Rug joins

Brian “Rug” Awadis joins the org roughly six months after launching his own channel. He'll become its biggest individual YouTuber and stay 13 years. [Wikipedia]

2013 · MLG Winter Championship: rough competitive debut

FaZe's first organized Call of Duty tournament appearance, and the team (Heist, Secretly, Folsom and ShamRoKz) secures a spot in the Championship Bracket at MLG Dallas on Mar 16 (https://x.com/FaZeClan/status/312819398140379136). The esports project is real now, and the results begin to follow. [Tubefilter]

2013 · FaZe Banks joins, from rival SoaR Gaming

Richard “Banks” Bengtson crosses over after leading SoaR's own trickshot/highlight channel. Later becomes COO, then CEO. [Wikitubia]

Mar 16, 2013 · #FAZE5 — the original recruitment challenge

564K views on the announcement. Seven years before the 2020 edition most people remember, FaZe runs the first #FAZE5: send clips, get shortlisted, get judged in public. A Top 100 update follows in April and a Top 20 montage in May (1.57M). [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Mar 25, 2013 · Clash of the Clan 3: North America vs Europe

1,065,214 views, edited by Gumi and Furran. The roster is now big enough and international enough to field two continents against each other. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2013 · First match against OpTic Gaming

A loss at MLG Spring Championship, but the start of what becomes Call of Duty esports' defining rivalry — one OpTic's own founder H3CZ later described as more mentorship than beef. [Dot Esports]

Jun 3–7, 2013 · The first #FAZE5 class joins, one a day for five days

Rockst4r (Jun 3), Tettoe (Jun 4), Hateful (Jun 5), GwidT (Jun 6) and Bazzles (Jun 7, with editor Ninjask added the same day) each get a full introduction video, staged across five consecutive days on the org's third anniversary week. Recruitment as episodic content — a format FaZe reruns in 2014 and again in 2020. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Aug 5, 2013 · “FaZe @ MLG Anaheim 2013”

463K views. The content side shows up at a professional LAN and vlogs it — months before the competitive side has a result worth reporting. FaZe covered esports as culture before it could compete in it. “FaZe @ MLG Columbus” follows in November (697K). [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Aug 9, 2013 · Spratt: EXAMPLE 4

1,527,029 views. The last of the truly enormous single-member sniping montages before the org's attention moves to esports rosters and, later, to lifestyle content. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Sept 2, 2013 · 3rd–4th at the PAX Prime CoD Invitational

A $1,250 finish — modest money, but the first result on the board after the MLG Winter Championship, and a sign the competitive side is finding its footing. [Esports Earnings]

Sept 19, 2013 · FaZe eSniping launches, led by WaRTeK

A dedicated European sniping/content division — the first seed of the international expansion that becomes FaZe France seven months later. [Wikipedia]

Nov 4, 2013 · The 2 Million Subscribers Teamtage

Edited by Gumi, 5.47M lifetime views, trailed since June (the trailer alone did 879K) in a months-long hype cycle that treated a montage drop like an album release. It is the second-biggest teamtage FaZe ever released — an earlier version of this page called it the biggest, before the 2012 1M teamtage's 12.1M turned up in the catalogue. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Nov 6, 2013 · Ghosts Teamtage #1

848K views, two days after the 2M teamtage — the game-specific sub-series format that runs alongside the numbered milestone teamtages. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Nov 29, 2013 · ACE and Enable join the competitive team

“A very large welcome to the newest members of the @FaZeCompetitive team,” FaZe posts of MLG ACE and Enable, the two players who become FaZe Red’s core. [FaZe Clan on X]

Dec 7, 2013 · #FAZE5 Top 20 absorbed into the roster

Banks posts that Adapt, Baz, Carl, GwidT, Kayos, Linkzy, Rockst4r, Sully and TeeTo all placed in the Top 20 for #FAZE5 and “have all joined FaZe.” It is the first public sign that the contest is a recruiting pipeline for the roster, not only a content series. [Banks on X]

Dec 26, 2013 · Christmas Teamtage, year two

Edited by MinK and Racky, 469K views, dropped the day after Christmas — the second in what becomes a five-year holiday run, every one landing Dec 24–26. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

03  2014-17 — YouTube house, then a CS:GO superteam

2014

Jan 6, 2014 · Introducing FaZe Adapt

356,649 views, edited by FaZe Ninja. This is the org's own introduction video for Adapt, and it settles a genuine reporting conflict: Streamscharts dates his join to 2011 and Dexerto to 2013, but the channel announced him in January 2014, and Banks had already posted in Dec 2013 that Adapt had placed in the #FAZE5 Top 20 and joined FaZe (https://x.com/Banks/status/409230065717608448). He goes on to direct FaZe's Call of Duty sniping division, and leaves in the Christmas 2025 exodus. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Jan 21, 2014 · Introducing FaZe Kay

1,075,920 views. Frazier Khattri joins, becomes one of the org's most visible UK creators, and is the member FaZe fires seven years later over the Save the Kids token. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2014 · First content house opens, in Plainview, NY

Temperrr and Banks set up shared living/content space, marking the pivot from pure trickshot channel to lifestyle brand. [Wikipedia]

Feb 27, 2014 · “The FaZe Application Process”

966,653 views. The org publishes how to get in — and turns its own hiring funnel into a content format that a million people watch. Nothing else in gaming was doing membership this transparently in 2014. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Mar 8, 2014 · FaZe qualify for Call of Duty Championship

FaZe qualify for the 2014 Call of Duty Championship, where they finish 6th three weeks later. [FaZe Clan on X]

Mar 30, 2014 · 6th at Call of Duty Championship 2014, $50,000

A real payday and a real result, though well short of the podium — a fair marker of where the competitive side stands one year after the MLG Winter Championship. [Esports Earnings]

Apr 3, 2014 · FaZe eSniping renamed FaZe France

The European sniping/content division, led by WaRTeK, gets its own identity as the org's first international expansion. [Wikipedia]

Apr 13, 2014 · #RELAPSE teamtage

Edited by Xero and Furran, 1.78M lifetime views. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Apr 20, 2014 · #FAZE5 returns, powered by G FUEL

480,805 views. The second edition, now with a title sponsor attached — the first time FaZe monetises its own recruitment. A Top 20 montage (837K) and a run of winner introductions follow through June. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

May 7, 2014 · ILLCAMS Episode 49, and then three years of silence

Featuring rapper Hoodie Allen for the second time, 2.33M views — a strong episode, though not the series record that this page previously claimed for it. Then it simply stopped. “Where is ILLCAMS 50?” became a running joke for three years; FaZe even tweeted that it was “Coming out TOMORROW” on Feb 6, 2016, then did not deliver it for another fifteen months. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

May 17, 2014 · “Player Profile: FaZe Censor”

414,797 views. The competitive Call of Duty roster gets the same treatment the trickshotters get — a produced player profile rather than a results post. FaZe is building personalities out of pros a full year before most orgs try it. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

May 24, 2014 · Introducing FaZe Jev

2.27M views on the intro, edited by FaZe Barker. Jason “Jev” Eugene brings a rage-commentary and sniping channel that grows to 5.4M subscribers and becomes one of the longest tenures in the org's history — roughly twelve years, outlasting nearly every name around him. He leaves on Jan 1, 2026, in the last wave of the exodus. Earlier reporting placed his join in August; the channel's own introduction video is May. [Dexerto]

Jun 2014 · The second #FAZE5 class arrives

Dirty, Brio, Bobo, NiQs and Agony are introduced across June, with a combined class video (1.03M) closing it out. Curtis “Dirty” Henke is still on the roster nine years later, and is one of the two names FaZe wants for its 2023 sniping revival. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Jul 6, 2014 · Wins the MLG 2K Series

A first-place finish that, paired with the Columbus Open win over OpTic later that year, makes 2014 the competitive side's best year yet. [Esports Earnings]

Oct 31, 2014 · FaZe Rain's 1 Million Subscribers montage

3.55M lifetime views, the biggest solo montage of the golden era and a reminder that individual members were becoming draws in their own right. [FaZe Rain on YouTube]

Nov 30, 2014 · Beats OpTic at the MLG Columbus Open

A marquee win over the chief rival, despite ACHES suffering a severe hand injury mid-tournament. [Dot Esports]

Nov 1, 2014 · #RED teamtage

A multi-Call-of-Duty teamtage edited by FaZe Barker, 4.55M views — second only to the 2M Subscribers Teamtage. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Dec 7, 2014 · FaZe Rug uploads his first prank video

His pivot from Call of Duty gameplay to prank and vlog content, the format that makes him the org's biggest individual YouTuber. [Wikipedia]

Dec 26, 2014 · Christmas Teamtage, year three

Edited by Racky, 340K views. Third year running, and the holiday drop is now an expectation rather than a one-off. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2015

Mar 2015 · FaZe Red take 3rd at the Call of Duty Championship

ACHES, SlasheR, Parasite and Enable, transferred over from the main roster, put FaZe on a Championship podium for the first time. It is the high point of the FaZe Red era. [Call of Duty Esports Wiki]

Apr 8, 2015 · ZooMaa joins FaZe for the first time

Tommy “ZooMaa” Paparatto signs for the first time and becomes the face of FaZe’s Call of Duty side for the next decade. He marks the ten-year anniversary on X in 2025 (152K views, 4.3K likes). [ZooMaa on X]

2015 · Hubrick investment brings in Lee Trink

Entrepreneur Sebastian Geurts invests via Hubrick and brings former Interscope Records executive Lee Trink on as an advisor. Trink later becomes CEO. [Wikipedia]

2015 · The Call of Duty side splits into FaZe Red and FaZe Black

Two rosters under one banner, with players moved between them mid-season. It doubles FaZe's shots at a bracket and dilutes the identity of both teams; the org repeats the split in 2016 before consolidating back to a single roster. [Call of Duty Esports Wiki]

May 26, 2015 · FaZe France's roster and staff walk out

The whole French division leaves, citing dissatisfaction with FaZe's plans for it, and goes on to found its own org. First real fracture of the international side. [Wikipedia]

2015 · Clayster joins the competitive Call of Duty roster

James “Clayster” Eubanks, already one of the scene's most decorated players, spends two seasons on FaZe before later winning CWL/CDL titles elsewhere. [Wikipedia]

2015 · Runner-up at MLG World Finals

Caps what multiple retrospectives call the org's most defining competitive year to date. [Grokipedia]

Aug 2 & Sept 20, 2015 · Two Call of Duty trophies in seven weeks, both taken off OpTic

FaZe beat OpTic 4–3 to win the Gfinity Summer Championship in London, then beat them again 4–1 to take MLG Pro League Season 3. The rivalry that began as a 2010 crossover video is now a final, twice in a summer, and FaZe win both. [Liquipedia]

Nov 5, 2015 · 3 Million Subscribers Teamtage

Edited by Agony, 1.75M views, trailed by a February teaser that itself pulled 750K. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Dec 26, 2015 · Black Ops 3 Holiday Teamtage — the biggest Christmas drop

Edited by Jebasu, 686K views, the highest-performing of the four holiday teamtages. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2016

Jan 2016 · Buys G2's CS:GO roster for ~$700K

Then the most expensive Counter-Strike team ever assembled: rain, jkaem, fox, Maikelele, aizy. FaZe's entry into a second major esport. [Liquipedia]

Mar 2016 · New CS:GO roster's major debut disappoints

Out in the first round at MLG Major Championship: Columbus, a rough return on a $700K investment. [Wikipedia]

Mar 7, 2016 · FaZe Apex: 5 Years in FaZe Clan

2.75M views. A member anniversary montage as a content format in its own right — the nostalgia play, a decade before it became strategy. [FaZe Apex on YouTube — upload no longer public]

Aug 6, 2016 · #TheReturn teamtage

4.02M views, the third-biggest teamtage ever and the last of the truly enormous ones. #TheRevival follows on Oct 15 (1.23M). [FaZe Clan YouTube]

Oct 2016 · karrigan joins as in-game leader

Finn “karrigan” Andersen stabilizes the CS:GO team's tactical direction. [Grokipedia]

Dec 24, 2016 · 2016 Holiday Teamtage — the last of the run

564K views, the fifth and final consecutive Christmas teamtage after 2012, 2013, 2014 and 2015. The tradition carries on in modified form as New Year's teamtages (Jan 1, 2018 — 254K; Jan 3, 2019 — 402K), then lapses entirely as the org pivots to Fortnite and lifestyle content. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2017

Feb 9, 2017 · NiKo signed from mousesports

A reported ~$500K buyout, a Counter-Strike record at the time. He plays one last event with mousesports before joining, and meaningfully lifts the roster's talent ceiling. [HLTV]

Mar 4, 2017 · #RECOVERY teamtage

685K views. The teamtage franchise is still running, at a fraction of its 2013–14 scale. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

May 30, 2017 · ILLCAMS 50 finally lands

Three years and three weeks after Episode 49, the most-requested video in FaZe history arrives and does 1.62M views. Two days later FaZe Jev — a member himself, not a bystander — posts “ILLCAMS 50 RANT” to his own channel, and it pulls 441K on its own. The drought had become a bigger story than the series, and the loudest voice on it came from inside the roster. [FaZe Clan YouTube]

2017 · Assembles a CS:GO “superteam”

Adds GuardiaN and olofmeister alongside NiKo, rain and karrigan — called the biggest talent consolidation in CS:GO history at the time. [Liquipedia]

2017 · The lifestyle side moves to LA and the “Clout House”

Banks relocates out of Plainview into the LA creator-house scene, founding the Clout Gang collective alongside FaZe. Exact date unconfirmed; the Clout House and FaZe's own houses were adjacent but separate operations. [Dexerto]

Aug 2017 · Public feud with Jake Paul's Team 10

An altercation involving a Team 10 member spirals into a YouTube beef. Banks gains roughly 1M subscribers over the following week; Paul loses about 200,000. [Wikitubia]

Oct 13–14, 2017 · Wins ELEAGUE CS:GO Premier, $500K

Fourteen years to the day after the first upload, Banks, OpTic's H3CZ and 100 Thieves' Nadeshot put all three orgs in one room for a Call of Duty LAN — Modern Warfare 2 and Black Ops 2, 4v4, with snipers-only brackets. The stars on the FaZe Esports crest

FaZe wears its championships the way a football club does. Four black stars appeared above the logo on the October 2025 crest; a fifth was added on 16 February 2026, the day after the Six Invitational final in Paris. FaZe has never published a key to them, so what follows is reconstructed from two things that can be checked: the crest files themselves, and the organisation's record. The reconstruction is exact — in the whole history of the organisation there are precisely four world championships before October 2025 and precisely five before February 2026, and the stars appear in step with them. Each one is a first place at the defining world event of its game, and each was won by a different roster.

★ One · Aug 22, 2021 — Call of Duty League Championship

Atlanta FaZe win the Call of Duty League Championship, eight years after the Call of Duty team's first competitive placings and two years after the org paid roughly $25 million for the franchise slot. The first world title in the organisation's history, in the game it was founded on. [Liquipedia]

★ Two · May 22, 2022 — PGL Major Antwerp

2–0 over Natus Vincere in the grand final for $500,000: karrigan, rain, broky, Twistzz and ropz take the only trophy in Counter-Strike that everyone agrees is a world championship. Nine months into a run that also took Katowice, ESL Pro League Season 15 and Cologne, and that ended in an Intel Grand Slam the following March. [Liquipedia]

★ Three · Oct 15, 2023 — Halo World Championship

4–0 over the defending champions OpTic in Seattle for $400,000, after a run through the lower bracket. The least-remembered of the five and the most improbable: it lands five weeks after the CEO is terminated and five days before the company agrees to sell itself, in a game the organisation had only entered in December 2021. [Halo Waypoint]

★ Four · Feb 16, 2025 — Six Invitational

3–1 over BDS for $1,000,000 of a $3,000,000 pool. Rainbow Six's world championship, won by the Brazilian roster the org has fielded since buying Team Fontt in 2018 — the division that came cheapest and has paid the most. [Liquipedia]

★ Five · Feb 15, 2026 — Six Invitational, again

3–1 over Team Secret in Paris for another $1,000,000, making FaZe the first organisation to defend a Six Invitational. The star goes up the next day. Eight weeks earlier the entire creator roster had walked out, which means the fifth star was won by the half of the company most people had stopped watching. [Liquipedia]

What is deliberately not up there. FaZe has won plenty that carries no star, and the omissions define the rule better than the inclusions do. The Esports World Cup titles are absent — Atlanta FaZe's Call of Duty win in Riyadh in August 2024, and the Rainbow Six win over FURIA in August 2026 — because the EWC is a cross-game festival rather than any one game's world championship. So is the 2023 Intel Grand Slam, the richest single cheque the org has ever taken at $1,000,000, because it is a cumulative award rather than an event. So are the Six Sweden Major of 2021, the ELEAGUE Premier of 2017, the CS Asia Championships of 2023 and the PUBG Global Championship runner-up finish of 2019. The bar is first place at the one event that decides a world champion, and nothing else clears it.

The sixth star that has not gone up. FaZe Vegas won the Call of Duty League Championship on 19 July 2026, which by the rule above should be a sixth star. It has not been added, and as of September 2026 the crest still carries five. The Call of Duty community has noticed. The likeliest explanation is structural rather than aesthetic: the Call of Duty franchise is owned and operated by Atlanta Esports Ventures with FaZe, while FaZe Esports — the entity whose crest this is — is wholly GameSquare's Counter-Strike and Rainbow Six operation. The first star is a Call of Duty title the org won when it still fully controlled that team. The sixth would be one it does not.

How this was established. By comparing the crest files themselves against the results record. The October 2025 mark carries four stars and the February 2026 mark carries five; the earlier logos — the 2024 crest and the rounded January 2025 rebrand — carry none. Liquipedia dates the two crest changes to 6 October 2025 and 16 February 2026, the second falling one day after the Six Invitational final. Placings, dates and prize figures come from Liquipedia's per-game pages for FaZe Clan, FaZe Vegas and the Halo roster. FaZe has published no star-by-star explanation, so the mapping above is an inference from the record rather than a statement from the organisation — but there is only one set of five results it can fit.

Notes on sources

How the early years were dated. Mainstream coverage of 2010–2014 is thin and mostly written from memory, so that stretch was rebuilt from FaZe’s own YouTube upload log — real upload dates and lifetime view counts for every video on the channel. Where the org’s own “Introducing FaZe X” video contradicts a later retrospective, the video is used. View counts are lifetime as of September 2026, not contemporaneous.

Precision. Entries dated to a month only are labelled that way. Three linked videos have since been removed from YouTube and point to the channel instead. Every entry is sourced to something publicly accessible — an article, filing, press release, public post or public video. Nothing here comes from internal documents.
